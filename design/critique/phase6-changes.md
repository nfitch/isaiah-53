## Mosiah differences and notes

Before, only verses 9 and 11 showed differences.

### Verse 1

- Differences shown: `+Yea +, +even +doth +not +Isaiah +say +: -? +,`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah opens with "Yea, even doth not Isaiah say:" before the verse. That line is Abinadi introducing the quotation, not part of Isaiah's text. Mosiah also joins the two questions with a comma where KJV has a question mark after "report."

### Verse 2

- Differences shown: `-a -: +; -,`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "out of dry ground" where KJV reads "out of a dry ground." Punctuation also differs (a semicolon for a colon, and no comma after "see him").

### Verse 3

- Differences shown: `-: +;`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Only punctuation differs: Mosiah has a semicolon where KJV has a colon after "grief."

### Verse 4

- Differences shown: `-hath +has -: +;`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "he has borne our griefs" where KJV reads "he hath borne our griefs." Punctuation also differs (a semicolon for a colon).

### Verse 5

- Differences shown: `-: +;`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Only punctuation differs: Mosiah has a semicolon where KJV has a colon after "iniquities."

### Verse 6

- Differences shown: `+, +, -iniquity +iniquities`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "the iniquities of us all" where KJV reads "the iniquity of us all." The Hebrew עֲוֹן is singular. Mosiah also sets off "like sheep" with commas.

### Verse 7

- Differences shown: `-: +; -, -openeth +opened`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "so he opened not his mouth" where KJV reads "so he openeth not his mouth." Punctuation also differs (a semicolon for a colon, and no comma after "dumb").

### Verse 8

- Differences shown: `-: +; -: +; -transgression +transgressions`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "the transgressions of my people" where KJV reads "the transgression of my people." The Hebrew פֶּשַׁע is singular. Punctuation also differs (semicolons for colons).

### Verse 9

- Differences shown: `-violence +evil`
- Note before: Mosiah reads "and he had done no evil" where KJV reads "because he had done no violence." The Hebrew חָמָס (khamas) means violence, wrong, injustice — the Book of Mormon rendering "no evil" is broader and less specific than either the Hebrew or the KJV.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "he had done no evil" where KJV reads "he had done no violence." The Hebrew חָמָס means violence or wrong.

### Verse 10

- Differences shown: `-: +; -,`
- Note before: Mosiah reads "when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin" — identical to KJV. However Mosiah adds no textual differences elsewhere. One notable point — the Book of Mormon text preserves the same ambiguity of "if" vs "when" as the KJV, rendering אִם as "when" throughout, identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Only punctuation differs: Mosiah has a semicolon where KJV has a colon after "grief," and no comma after "sin."

### Verse 11

- Differences shown: `-of -: +;`
- Note before: Mosiah reads "he shall see the travail of his soul and shall be satisfied" — inserting "the travail of his soul" as the explicit object of "he shall see," where the Hebrew leaves the object unstated and KJV supplies it from context. Both KJV and Mosiah resolve the ambiguity the same way, but the Hebrew leaves he shall see standing alone.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "He shall see the travail of his soul" where KJV reads "He shall see of the travail of his soul." Punctuation also differs (a semicolon for a colon).

### Verse 12

- Differences shown: `-: +; -bare +bore -sin +sins`
- Note before: Identical to KJV — no differences.
- Note after: Mosiah reads "he bore the sins of many" where KJV reads "he bare the sin of many." The Hebrew חֵטְא is singular. Punctuation also differs (a semicolon for a colon).
